# Domain Glossary

## Participant

A person identified by a Clerk user account who may apply to join the hackathon.

## Application

One attempt by a participant to enter the hackathon. A rejected application remains part of the participant's history; applying again creates a new application.

## Active application

An application that is being drafted, awaiting a decision, or has been accepted. A participant may have only one active application at a time.

## Acceptance details

Personal and logistical information requested only after an application is accepted. Completing these details confirms the participant's attendance information.
